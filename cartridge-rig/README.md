# Cartridge rig

Film transport test rig with a removable cartridge. The rings, throat rollers
and both spools sit in a cartridge (base plate + two posts + cap). You wrap the
film around the outside of the cartridge, lower it into the body and fix the
cap with two thumbscrews.

Design: `cartridge-rig.sc` (SolidCraft). Renders: `cartridge.png` (cartridge
out of the body), `section.png` (section through the assembly).

## Assembly notes

- Press a 623 bearing into each end of both throat rollers.
- Bottom ring: lay it on the base, then fit the three bearings on their M3 × 8
  screws with a washer on top. The ring's inner ledge sits under the bearings,
  so nothing falls out.
- Top ring: snap it over the keeper collar on the underside of the cap. If it
  is too tight, sand the keeper edge lightly.
- Thumb knobs: press an M3 × 12 screw head into each knob pocket.
- Cartridge: put spools and rollers in, screw rods into the base, lay the cap on
  and fix it with the two M3 × 10 screws into the posts and the rod nuts.
- Knobs: push the spool shafts up through the floor, felt washer under the
  supply knob, press the knobs on.

## Loading

1. Undo the two thumbscrews (2–3 turns; leave them in the cap) and lift the
   cartridge out by the cap.
2. Thread the leader from the supply spool round its roller, wrap the film
   around the outside of the rings (between the ring flanges) and back through
   the other roller to the take-up spool.
3. Turn the take-up spool by its flange until the film is taut; hold the supply
   spool with a finger (there is no brake outside the body).
4. Lower the cartridge into the body. If it stops about 3 mm short, turn the
   knobs until the keys drop into the spool slots.
5. Tighten the thumbscrews.
